\ReportNotice{Template only. Replace every placeholder before publishing. No measurement or hardware action is claimed.}
\section{Outcome}
State the result first. Distinguish proposed, attempted, owner-confirmed and adopted changes.
\section{Scope and conditions}
Record laser identity, task/job IDs, operating mode and revision, current owner policy revision,
model and effort, instruction revision, objective, permissions and exclusions.
\section{Data and timing}
Identify source files and queries, acquisition clock, local timezone, relative time origin,
sampling rate, missing/invalid points, and the interval actually evaluated.
\section{Method}
Describe the baseline, candidate selection, suggested step sizes, actual applied steps,
comparison windows and interruptions. Suggestions are not hard limits or permission.

\section{Limitations and next action}
Separate unresolved questions, deferred online checks and required human decisions.

List source revisions, immutable artifact hashes, analysis/build commands and output filenames.
